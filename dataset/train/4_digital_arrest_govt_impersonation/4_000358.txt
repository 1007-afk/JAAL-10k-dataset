caller: Namaste sir, mai Inspector Singh bol raha hoon, CBI se. Aapke naam par ek parcel aaya hai, jo illegal maal se bhara hua hai.
receiver: Kya? Illegal maal? Mera naam to aisa kuch nahi hai. Mai toh bas ghar par baitha tha.
caller: Sir, hamare records ke according parcel aapke address par aaya tha. Usmein suspicious items mile hain, jo drug trafficking se related hain. Hamko FIR register karne se pehle aapka statement chahiye.
receiver: Lekin mai toh kuch order nahi kiya tha. Kisi ne galat address diya hoga. Mai abhi police station jaa sakta hoon, apna statement dene ke liye.
caller: Sir, police station jaane ki zarurat nahi hai. Hamko bas thoda sa information chahiye, aur 50,000 rupay ka fine jama karne ke liye receipt. Yeh fine aapki reputation bachayega aur case ko close kar dega.
receiver: Paanch hazar rupay? Yeh thora zyada lag raha hai. Mai abhi koi payment nahi kar sakta. Hamara bank account check karo, koi mistake toh nahi hai na?
caller: Sir, yeh process hai. Legal fees aur investigation charges shamil hain. Jaldi kijiye, warna FIR register ho jayega aur aapko arrest bhi kiya ja sakta hai. Aapka naam international criminal list mein daal diya jayega.
