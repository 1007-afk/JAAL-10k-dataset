caller: Namaste sir, mein Indian Customs se bol raha hoon. Aapke naam par ek parcel Gujarat mein mila hai jo illegal items se bhara hua hai.
receiver: Kya? Mere naam par? Main toh kabhi Gujarat nahi gaya. Yeh kya bakwaas hai?
caller: Sir, records mein aapka naam aur address hai. Parcel mein suspicious items hain, jaisi ki banned drugs. Aapko is matter mein involve kiya jaa raha hai.
receiver: Drugs? Main toh ek sadha insaan hoon! Yeh koi dhoka hai. Mujhe yeh parcel nahi mila hai, main police mein report karunga.
caller: Sir, police mein report karne se yeh matter aur bhi serious ho jayega. Aapko Rs. 50,000 ka fine bhar kar baat samadhen ko padegi, taki parcel ko release kar sakein aur aapka naam clear ho sakein.
receiver: 50,000? Yeh toh loot hai! Main paisa nahi de sakta. Aapko sunna chahiye, yeh ek scam hai! Main abhi police ko call karta hoon.
caller: Sir, jaldi karo. Agar aap fine nahi bharenge, toh hum aapke khilaf warrant nikal denge aur aapko arrest bhi kar sakte hain. Aapka record kharab ho jayega aur jail bhi ho sakti hai.
receiver: Jail? Bhai, abhi meine chutti li hai. Maine kabhi kuch galti nahi ki. Mein koi bhi paisa nahi de raha.
caller: Sir, ye aapki final opportunity hai. Aapka naam, aapka future, sab kharab ho jayega.
receiver: Mujhe koi interest nahi. Main abhi police mein complaint karunga aur aapka number bhi public karunga.
