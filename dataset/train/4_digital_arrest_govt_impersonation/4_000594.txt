caller: Namaste, sir. This is Inspector Sharma from the Cyber Crime Investigation Cell, Mumbai. We need to speak with you urgently regarding a suspicious transaction linked to your bank account.
receiver: Inspector sahab? Kya hua? Kuch problem hai kya? I was just checking my account online, everything seemed fine.
caller: Sir, our systems detected a large sum of money being transferred from your account to an unknown overseas account. It appears to be linked to a case of international money laundering. We need to verify some details to secure your funds and prevent further fraudulent activity. Can you please provide me with the last four digits of your account number and the OTP you just received on your phone?
receiver: Wait a minute! OTP? Why do you need the OTP? Shouldn't I be calling the bank myself if there’s a problem with my account? And why are you calling from this number? It doesn't look like the official Mumbai Police number.
caller: Sir, time is of the essence. The fraudsters are very clever. Providing the OTP will allow us to freeze the account and trace the funds immediately. The official number is currently undergoing maintenance. Please, cooperate and provide the OTP so we can protect your money. We also need a recent copy of your PAN card to cross-reference the details.
receiver: No, I’m not giving you any OTP or PAN card details. This sounds like a scam. I’m going to call my bank directly and report this. You are clearly not from the police.
caller: Sir, don't be foolish! This is a serious matter. If you don't cooperate, we’ll have to file a formal complaint against you for aiding and abetting money laundering. We have your IP address and can track your location. Your family's safety could be at risk. Give us the OTP now!
