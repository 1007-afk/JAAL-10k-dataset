receiver: ₹2800? Flipkart se mujhe toh kuch order nahi kiya tha. Yeh kya ho raha hai?

caller: Sir, this is standard procedure to prevent fraud. It's a one-time verification. Without it, we’ll have to return the parcel to Flipkart, and you’ll lose the washing machine. The OTP is 1947.
receiver: Ek minute! Main police se report karunga. Yeh scam hai!
