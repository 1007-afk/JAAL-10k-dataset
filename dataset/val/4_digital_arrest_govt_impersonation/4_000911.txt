caller: Namaste, sir. This is Inspector Sharma calling from the Directorate of Enforcement, Mumbai. We’ve detected some unusual activity linked to your bank account – several international transactions flagged as potentially related to online gambling and money laundering.
receiver: What? Gambling? I don’t gamble! This must be a mistake. Can you tell me more? My account number is private.
caller: Sir, we understand this is concerning. It’s standard procedure. We just need to verify your identity and cross-reference some details. A recent transaction involved a cryptocurrency exchange, CoinSpark. Have you ever heard of it? We need you to confirm the last four digits of your PAN card for verification.
receiver: CoinSpark? No, I’ve never used that. My PAN card? That seems a bit odd to ask over the phone. Shouldn’t I be getting this in writing?
caller: Sir, time is of the essence. We have to act swiftly to prevent further financial crimes. This is a matter of national security. Just the last four digits, sir, to confirm your identity. Failure to cooperate could lead to further investigation and potential legal repercussions. Please, just give me the last four digits of your PAN card, it is for your safety.
receiver: Wait a minute… legal repercussions? This is starting to feel very fishy. I’m going to hang up now and contact the Directorate of Enforcement directly to confirm this call.
caller: Sir, don’t be hasty! This is a sensitive matter, and ending the call will only delay the process. Your cooperation is crucial. Please, sir, just those four digits… We can resolve this quickly.
