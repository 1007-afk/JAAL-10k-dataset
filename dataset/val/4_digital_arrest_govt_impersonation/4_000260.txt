caller: Namaste, is this Mrs. Sharma? This is Inspector Rajan from the Delhi Customs Department. We have a serious issue regarding a parcel registered under your name.
receiver: Haan? Parcel? Mujhe koi parcel nahi mila. Mein toh ghar par hoon.
caller: Madam, according to our records, a parcel containing suspicious items has been intercepted at the Delhi airport. It’s linked to your address. To clear it, we need to verify some details. This is a very sensitive matter.
receiver: Suspicious items? Kya items? Mein toh kabhi parcel nahi order kiya. Yeh kuch ghalat ho raha hai.
caller: Madam, we understand your concern, but we need to act quickly. The parcel contains undeclared electronics, potentially related to online fraud. To avoid legal complications and further investigation, a small payment of ₹15,000 is required to expedite the clearance process. Please transfer it to the account number I’ll provide.
receiver: ₹15,000? Online fraud? Kaise? Mein bank account number nahi de sakti. Yeh scam lag raha hai.
caller: Madam, this is not a scam. This is a standard procedure to confirm your identity and prevent any further issues. If you refuse to cooperate, we will have to involve the legal department, which will be much more costly.
receiver: Legal department? ₹15,000 bhi bahut zyada hai. Main police mein complaint karungi. (Hangs up)
