caller: Namaste sir, mai Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) se bol raha hoon. Aapke naam par ek parcel customs mein ruk gaya hai, jismein illegal items paye gaye hain.
receiver: Kya? Mere naam par? Main toh kabhi parcel order nahi kiya. Kya items paye gaye hain?
caller: Sir, parcel mein suspicious electronic devices paye gaye hain, jo drug trafficking mein use ho sakte hain. Aapko is matter mein involved mana ja raha hai.
receiver: Yeh bilkul galat hai! Main drug trafficking ya kisi illegal kaam se judaa nahi hoon. Mujhe koi parcel nahi mila hai.
caller: Sir, humara record kehti hai ki parcel aapke address se aaya hai. Case ko close karne aur aapko pareshani se bachane ke liye, aapko ₹50,000 ka fine bharvan hoga. Video call par aaiye aur baat karte hain.
receiver: ₹50,000? Sir, yeh thoda zyada lag raha hai. Agar aap serious hain toh mujhe koi official reference number ya document number de sakte hain? Main apne advocate se bhi baat karna chahtunga.
caller: Sir, time bahut kam hai. Agar aap fine nahi bharen toh humko compulsion hoga ki humko case register karna padega aur aapko arrest karna padega. Video call par aaiye, main aapko procedure bataunga aur aapko convince karunga. Aapka future at stake hai sir.
receiver: Sir, mujhe yeh scam lag raha hai. Main koi bhi payment nahi karunga aur aapko koi personal information bhi nahi dunga. Main abhi police mein report karunga aur apne advocate se baat karta hoon. Is conversation ko record kar raha hoon.
