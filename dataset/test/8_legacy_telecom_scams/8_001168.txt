receiver: Kya? ₹15,000? Seriously? UPI ID pooch rahe ho? Wait karo, ye to abhi tot gaya hai! Mujhe kuch samajh mein nahin aa raha! Kaise jeeta?
caller: Arre bhai, yeh to bas shukriya hai aapki! 'Shine Bright Lottery' ne aapko luckly draw mein select kiya hai. UPI ID sirf aapke money ko fast track karne ke liye hai, warna delay ho sakti hai. Aap tension mat le.
receiver: Tension mat le? Haan, okay. Par mujhe meri name aur aapki company ka naam batao. Main koi bhi stranger ko apni banking details nahin deta, samajh rahi ho na? Agar aapki company ka naam aur meri name bataayenge, toh hi mein aage badhenge.
